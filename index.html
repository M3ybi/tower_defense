How the game works
Quick rules and scoring clarity
  • RED marker: primary targets. Destroy them for best results.
  • GREEN marker: distractors. They exist to break your rhythm.
  • Episodes: waves of enemies. Your mission runs through all episodes.
  • Episode duration: time window enemies stay active before cleanup.
  • Variance: adds randomness per wave for replayability.

Scoring and fairness
Scores are computed server-side from shots/hits. Standard runs are normalized by expected totals per level so changing settings does not create incomparable scores. Runs with custom settings are excluded by default.
Breakdown (v2): Hit score + Level bonus + Accuracy + Completion + Volume + Variance (custom penalty) multiplied by a Level multiplier.
